# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Customer Champions** — Joveo's unified role title for all Customer Success team members.

Two cohorts with distinct needs:

- **Newbies (first 90 days):** Mandatory foundational curriculum delivered as a sequential waterfall — Module → Case Study → Failure Story — with each chapter locked until the previous is complete. They need progressive disclosure, clear progress signals, and a sense of safe, guided onboarding.
- **Veterans (post-3-month track):** Personalized learning paths driven by AI gap analysis and their individual Bloom's Taxonomy level (Apply → Analyse → Evaluate → Create). They need recommendations that respect their expertise, not content that feels remedial.

Both cohorts use the platform during working hours alongside live client responsibilities — it is a job aid first, an LMS second.

## Product Purpose

A three-page internal platform that centralizes the institutional knowledge currently locked in expert heads and scattered documents, making it accessible, learnable, and practicable for every Customer Champion:

- **The Workspace** — operational intelligence hub: pre-meeting radar, Knowledge Oracle search, post-meeting Ego Trap analysis, account Mission Briefings.
- **The Library** — async learning surface: AI-generated personalized modules, living case studies on real ongoing accounts, a Failure Library of churned account post-mortems.
- **The Simulator** — high-stakes practice arena: AI-powered roleplay scenarios, Blind Diagnosis challenges, QBR Builder & Defense, post-mortem reconstruction.

**Success looks like:** Every Customer Champion performs as effectively as the best one on the team, faster — regardless of seniority or geography. Knowledge transfer stops depending on osmosis and tribal memory.

## Brand Personality

Warm, encouraging, clear.

The emotional register is a great mentor: approachable, never condescending, makes complex situations feel navigable. It earns trust by being genuinely useful, not by looking impressive. The tone is the colleague who gives you the real talk before a hard meeting — calm, specific, on your side.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS (Notion, Linear):** Neutral-gray minimal everything. No whitespace as personality here — this is a tool for real work, not design portfolio bait.
- **Corporate LMS (Workday, SAP SuccessFactors):** Stiff enterprise training portals — dense tables, blue gradients, icon clipart, "click Next to continue." This platform must feel like nothing those systems feel like.
- **Consumer edtech (Duolingo, Coursera):** Gamification overload, bright primary color confetti, streak badges, and infantilizing copy. The users are professionals managing client relationships; the design respects that.
- **Dashboard overload (Salesforce, HubSpot):** Cramming metrics, chart widgets, and data panels onto every screen. The Workspace is not a dashboard — it is a focused, contextual view of right-now information.

## Design Principles

1. **Mentor, not manager.** Every surface speaks like a trusted colleague who's on your side — helpful, honest, specific. When AI surfaces a coaching moment (Ego Trap, Stagnant Gap Alert), it frames it as "let's try differently" not "you underperformed." The system is never punitive.

2. **Earn the next layer.** Start with the essential action; reveal depth as the user advances. A newbie's Workspace shows a focused waterfall. A veteran sees gap analysis and autonomous pathways. The UI's complexity mirrors the user's Bloom's level — it grows with them.

3. **Respect the clock.** Customer Champions are in client calls and account reviews all day. Every interaction must be fast to enter, fast to exit, and meaningfully useful within the time available. Weekly Radar activates before meetings. Ego Trap fires while the call is fresh. The platform earns attention; it doesn't demand it.

4. **Safe to fail.** Especially in the Simulator, but everywhere: the design must make it psychologically safe to not know something, to answer wrong, to revisit a topic. No shame mechanics. Progress indicators celebrate movement, not just perfection.

5. **Show the work.** When AI generates content, flags a knowledge gap, or makes a recommendation, surface just enough reasoning to make it credible — without burying the action. "This module was built from your last 3 missed deals in the pharma vertical" is more useful than a generic recommendation card.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Keyboard navigation throughout, proper semantic HTML, color is never the sole carrier of meaning (especially for Bloom's level and completion state indicators). Reduced-motion alternative required for all animations — the platform runs during a workday; no seizure or distraction risk. Light and dark themes both meet AA contrast minimums independently.
