### FINDINGS

#### 1. The Gemma judge is told the SINK pairs span the UNION population's distinct calls — the evidence line is arithmetically impossible for 5 of 9 clusters, and every one of those 5 got a non-sink verdict

- **File:line**: `Brain/calibration/diagnose_sink_pool.py:288-290` (`_render_cluster`), built at `:218-219` (`_build_cluster_records`)
- **What is wrong**: `distinct_calls` and `call_coverage` are computed over `members` — sink members **plus** the volume-matched control members. `_render_cluster` then renders them as
  `EVIDENCE: {n_sink} discarded pair(s) in this cluster, spanning {distinct_calls} distinct call(s) ({call_coverage:.0%} of the corpus)`.
  A pair belongs to exactly one call, so distinct calls over the sink members can never exceed `n_sink`. From the committed artifact: cluster_2 `n_sink=10` → "28 distinct calls"; cluster_7 `22` → `28`; cluster_6 `31` → `53`; cluster_3 `61` → `85`; cluster_8 `81` → `113`. The control was introduced precisely so it would *not* be shown to the judge (`:205-208` samples deliberately from sink members only) — and then its call coverage is fed in anyway, wearing the sink pool's label.
  This is not a cosmetic mislabel: `PROMPT_SINK_POOL_TRIAGE` (`shared/prompts.py:963`) names it as a judging criterion — *"Distinct-call support is evidence of a RECURRING move rather than a one-off. Low support is a reason to look harder, not an automatic rejection."*
- **What number it corrupts, and in WHICH DIRECTION**: **inflates** the recurrence evidence for every mixed cluster, biasing the three-way verdict away from `genuine_sink` and toward `belongs_to_existing` / `new_coachable_topic`. The pattern in the artifact matches: the two clusters where inflation is impossible or negligible (cluster_1 535 sink / 287 calls; cluster_4 74 / 78) are the **only two** `genuine_sink` verdicts; all five arithmetically-impossible ones are non-sink verdicts. It corrupts (a) the headline `genuine_sink 2/609, belongs_to_existing 5/199, new_coachable_topic 2/175` split, (b) the two "homeless topic" support figures quoted verbatim in CLAUDE.md — `strategic_performance_consulting (94 pairs / 115 calls)` and `technical_operational_alignment (81/113)`, where the call counts are union counts and the true sink-only ceilings are 94 and 81 — and (c) downstream, the `by_cluster` arm of `replay_layer_c_admitted.py`, which routes exactly the `belongs_to_existing` clusters.
  The same conflation appears in the human report at `:412-414` (`size=… (sink=… control=…) … calls={distinct_calls} coverage=…`).
- **Confidence**: **certain** for the code defect and the arithmetic impossibility; **likely** for the verdict-direction consequence (the correlation with verdict is 5/5 but n=9).
- **Cheapest way to verify**: free, no DB —
  `python -c "import json;p=json.load(open('Brain/artifacts/sink_pool_clusters.json',encoding='utf-8-sig'));[print(c['id'],c['n_sink'],c['distinct_calls'],p['verdicts'][c['id']]['verdict']) for c in p['clusters']]"`

#### 2. `merged` was split out of the outcome enum but is still pooled into the support-thickening statistic — the exact metric the merge-blindness bug manufactured

- **File:line**: `Brain/calibration/replay_layer_c_admitted.py:354-357`, reported at `:374-377` and printed at `:403-409`
- **What is wrong**: `_match_milestones` correctly emits four outcomes, but `_summarise_arm` collects `support_frac_deltas` for `o["outcome"] in ("matched", "merged")` — a two-way collapse of the four-way enum — and `support_thickened` / `support_thinned` / `support_frac_delta_mean` are computed over that pooled list. A `merged` row's `arm_support` is by construction the support of a cluster that absorbed 2+ baseline milestones, so it is guaranteed larger. The report's own disclaimer (`:400-402`) explains that `merged` is destructive and that support is now a fraction — and then prints a `thick`/`thin` column that still counts the merges.
- **What number it corrupts, and in WHICH DIRECTION**: **inflates** `support_thickened` and `support_frac_delta_mean`, which is the statistic whose earlier inflated version was read as "evidence thickening" and drove the wrong `by_cluster` recommendation. Computed from `layer_c_admitted_replay_postfix.json`:

  | arm | matched rows | merged rows |
  |---|---|---|
  | `by_cluster` | n=375, thick=6, mean **+0.0007** | n=8, thick=7, mean **+0.4587** |
  | `by_trigger_nonsink` | n=172, thick=59, mean **−0.0170** | n=83, thick=78, mean **+0.3825** |

  The published `by_cluster` figure `support_thickened=13, support_frac_delta_mean=+0.0102` is essentially *entirely* the 8 merges; the 375 genuinely matched milestones move by +0.0007, i.e. nothing. In `by_trigger_nonsink` the pooling actively reverses the sign — cleanly matched milestones **thin** by −0.017 while the printed arm mean is +0.113. So the one arm-level statistic that survived the merge fix still reports merges as thickening.
- **Confidence**: **certain** (code + recomputed from the persisted artifact).
- **Cheapest way to verify**: free — split `payload["detail"][arm][key]["outcomes"]` by `o["outcome"]` and take `mean(arm_support_frac − base_support_frac)` per group; compare to `payload["arms"][…]["support_frac_delta_mean"]`.

#### 4. The placebo is matched on CLAUSE volume but the support gate is keyed on DISTINCT CALLS — and in the arm the conclusion turns on, the placebo perturbs calls 2.3× harder than the treatment

- **File:line**: `Brain/calibration/replay_layer_c_admitted.py:163-179` (`_placebo_pool`), consumed at `:566-569`; gate at `:239-240` via `cluster_evidence.required_milestone_support`
- **What is wrong**: `_placebo_pool` draws donors until `total_clauses >= n_clauses_wanted`. Admitted sink pairs concentrate in calls the scenario already contains; the placebo's donors are drawn uniformly at random across the whole corpus, so nearly every donor pair brings a *new* call. Matching on clauses therefore does not match on the quantity the gate reads.
  Recomputed from `layer_c_admitted_replay_postfix.json` (arm `scenario_calls` recovered as `support / support_frac`):

  | arm | extra clauses T / P | new distinct calls T / P |
  |---|---|---|
  | `by_trigger_nonsink` | 9,339 / 9,701 | 802 / 805 — matched |
  | `by_response` | 11,538 / 11,920 | 822 / 823 — matched |
  | **`by_cluster`** | **1,952 / 1,991** | **63 / 144 — 2.3×** |

  Per scenario it is worse: `ai_capability_discovery` goes 55 → **76** calls under treatment and 55 → **134** under placebo on an *identical* 853 admitted clauses, i.e. required support 8 vs 14. `media_channel_and_retargeting_discovery` additionally overshoots on clauses (79 vs 108, +37%) because the loop breaks only after crossing the target.
- **What number it corrupts, and in WHICH DIRECTION**: **inflates the placebo's `lost` count** for `by_cluster` specifically, which is the load-bearing published comparison ("beats its own placebo on lost, 1 vs 6"). The placebo's 6 losses are spread 1/1/2/2 across exactly the four scenarios where it faces the stiffer gate. Part of the 6-vs-1 gap is a structural artifact of how the placebo was drawn, not of content quality. The two rejected arms are unaffected (802/805, 822/823) so their rejection stands.
- **Confidence**: **certain** for the volume mismatch (recomputed); **likely** for how much of the 6-vs-1 gap it explains (the exact per-milestone attribution needs a re-run, which is out of scope here).
- **Cheapest way to verify**: free — the recomputation above, from `detail[arm][key]["outcomes"][i]["arm_support"] / ["arm_support_frac"]`.

#### 5. The placebo is a *dispersed* null, while the metric it controls is *"did the injected content form a cluster"* — the treatment's content is coherent by construction

- **File:line**: `Brain/calibration/replay_layer_c_admitted.py:169` (`donors = [… for k, ps in … if k != scenario_key for p in ps]`, then `rng.shuffle`)
- **What is wrong**: the docstring's stated design is "topically wrong content at the right volume". But the treatment's admitted pairs are not merely *right*-topic — under `by_cluster` they are literally the members of a single HDBSCAN cluster of response embeddings produced by `diagnose_sink_pool.py`, i.e. mutually coherent by selection. The placebo's donors are drawn uniformly across every other coachable scenario, i.e. maximally dispersed. `gained_majority_admitted` counts new arm clusters whose clauses are >50% injected — which is a coherence test. The null controls volume and leaves coherence free, on the one axis the metric measures.
- **What number it corrupts, and in WHICH DIRECTION**: **inflates** the treatment/placebo gain gap. In `by_cluster` the placebo scores 0 majority-admitted gains while producing **15** `gained_other` clusters against the treatment's 6 — i.e. the placebo *did* re-partition the pool, its donors just never formed a majority-donor cluster. A coherent-but-wrong-topic null (all donors drawn from one other scenario) would be the matched control.
  Honest counter-evidence, stated because it weakens the finding: `ai_capability_discovery` received the single largest coherent injection (853 clauses, one whole cluster) and gained **0**; and in `by_trigger_nonsink` the placebo out-gains the treatment 98 vs 84. So the bias is real but not deterministic.
- **Confidence**: **likely**.
- **Cheapest way to verify**: free — compare `gained_other` (computed at `:359-362`, stored, **never printed**) between each treatment and its placebo in the postfix artifact.

#### 11. `check_milestone_thickening.py` prints only support INCREASES, and its automated dilution indicator has a denominator that grows with the treatment — so it structurally cannot fire

- **File:line**: `Brain/calibration/check_milestone_thickening.py:112-116` and `:123`
- **What is wrong**: two independent problems in the script that was built to settle thickening-vs-dilution.

(a) and (b) both **deflate** the appearance of harm; (c) means the harm that was actually present is representable in the output only as *"three THICKENED blocks whose AFTER clause lists happen to be identical"*, which a reader must notice unaided.
- **Confidence**: **certain** for the code; the historical consequence is already recorded in CLAUDE.md.
- **Cheapest way to verify**: read `:123` and `:112`; note there is no `claimed` set anywhere in the file.

### CLEAN

Categories I checked and found **correct**, with the reason:

- **The `merged` fix itself is genuinely present and correct in `_match_milestones`** (`replay_layer_c_admitted.py:289-341`). `claims` accumulates every baseline index whose `best_frac > 0.5` per arm-cluster index; `merged_arm_idx` is the >1 set; `outcome_name` branches on it *before* the outcome is appended; and `n_baseline_in_same_cluster` is carried into the record. The consistency check reproduces exactly against the two persisted artifacts: `matched + merged` post-fix equals the pre-fix `matched` in all three arms (172+83 = 255, 172+92 = 264, 375+8 = 383).
